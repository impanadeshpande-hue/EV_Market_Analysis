# 🚗 Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Analysis Dashboard
# 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes Electric Vehicle (EV) market trends across India by examining sales performance, manufacturer market share, vehicle categories, charging infrastructure, and regional adoption patterns.The project provides actionable insights for manufacturers, policymakers, and stakeholders to understand EV growth and identify future opportunities.

# 🎯 Objectives
1.Analyze EV sales trends across states and cities.
2.Identify top-performing manufacturers.
3.Evaluate charging infrastructure availability.
4.Compare vehicle categories and battery performance.
5.Monitor market share and revenue contribution.
5.Forecast future EV market growth.
